FootSal Privacy Policy

Modern Prophet Labs · Effective 10 November 2026

In short: FootSal does not collect, share or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy covers the FootSal app for Android (package in.mplabs.footsal), published by Modern Prophet Labs.

Data we collect

None. FootSal has no servers of its own and sends nothing about you or how you play to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices between games, FootSal saves a few things in the app's own storage on your phone:

  • your settings (sound, music, camera, graphics, clock and haptics);
  • which tutorial tips you have already seen;
  • the team name you choose for single player.

This never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it.

Internet access

FootSal plays fully offline. It only goes online in two cases, both started by you:

  • High graphics. If you choose High in Settings, the app downloads sharper texture and sky images from Poly Haven, a free public library of 3D assets. As with any download, Poly Haven's servers see your device's IP address and the files requested. FootSal sends nothing else. The Standard setting downloads nothing.
  • Links. Links on the Credits screen open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the optional High graphics downloads described above.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

FootSal is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

The web version

FootSal can also be played on ankitpassi.in. That website may use optional analytics cookies, but only if you accept them in its cookie banner. This policy covers the Android app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
