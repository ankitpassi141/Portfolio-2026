Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · SiftHappens for Windows · Made by Modern Prophet Labs

In short: SiftHappens works entirely on your PC. It does not upload your photos, it does not collect or send any personal information, and it has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What SiftHappens does

SiftHappens looks at the photos in a folder you choose (including its subfolders) and sorts them into buckets such as Duplicates, Screenshots, Receipts, Forwards & Junk and Everything Else. When you ask it to, it moves or copies the photos into folders on your PC. Video files it finds are moved or copied into a Videos folder without being opened or analysed.

Information it reads, and where that happens

To sort your photos, SiftHappens reads them on your PC only:

  • the image itself, to measure things like sharpness, brightness and similarity to other photos;
  • the photo’s file name, folder, size and dates;
  • camera details stored inside the photo (EXIF), such as the camera model, the date it was taken and, if present, its location.

All of this is processed on your device. None of it is sent anywhere. SiftHappens uses no artificial intelligence services and no cloud processing.

What it keeps on your PC

So it can resume your work and re-scan folders quickly, SiftHappens saves a few files in its own app data folder in your Windows user profile:

  • small preview thumbnails of the photos it has scanned, and the measurements it took;
  • your current review (which photo is in which bucket) and your bucket settings, including any buckets and rules you create;
  • a record of the last time it moved or copied photos, so you can undo it.

These files never leave your PC. Uninstalling SiftHappens removes them. Starting from scratch in the app clears the saved review.

Your files

SiftHappens only reads the folder you pick and only changes it when you choose “Put into folders”. It never deletes photos. Every move or copy can be undone from the app.

Internet access

SiftHappens works offline and does not connect to the internet on its own. Two things open outside the app, only when you click them: the “Made by Modern Prophet Labs” link opens ankitpassi.in in your web browser, and, if your PC cannot read iPhone (HEIC) photos, a link opens Microsoft’s free HEIF and HEVC extensions in the Microsoft Store. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not collect your information, so we do not sell, share or rent it to anyone.

Microsoft Store

If you get SiftHappens from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, updates and any purchase under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your personal details from that process beyond what Microsoft provides to publishers in aggregate.

Children

SiftHappens does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are unlikely to affect you.
